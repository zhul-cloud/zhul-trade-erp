## Why

The five supply-chain SOP workbooks currently use repeated generic checklist text, so BAU, Quality, and Stage Gate do not express the decisions required by each supply-chain stage. The workbooks must become operationally useful and remain aligned with the supply-chain lifecycle map.

## What Changes

- Rewrite the five workbooks so their Stage sheets use the lifecycle map as the single stage dictionary.
- Provide stage-specific BAU Checklist, Quality Checklist, and Stage Gate content for all 25 map nodes.
- Remove residual customer-sales workflow text from supply-chain workbook content.
- Preserve the existing customer SOP workbook layout, styles, and supporting tabs.
- Add validation that compares workbook stage codes and names with the lifecycle map dictionary.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `supply-chain-sop`: Stage-specific operating checklists and gates for the supply-chain lifecycle.
